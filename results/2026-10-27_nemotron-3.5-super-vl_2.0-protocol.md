## Findings

1. **Weak model; the tier matches Nemotron 3 Nano.** Legacy leaderboard score 9 (vs. Nano's 11); the
   N=3 mean of 13 is in the same bottom tier. Recall is respectable for a free model (14/50
   across the union), but precision is poor: 47% of v1 findings are FP.
2. **Fixed frame budget regardless of clip length.** Prompt tokens are ~2,860–3,100 for *every*
   clip, including the 2-minute `_long-scene` (only +34 tokens over a 10 s clip). The provider
   samples a fixed handful of frames per video. Many FPs are the signature of sparse sampling:
   "skips the landing phase", "bowl appears already mid-pour", "hand jumps instantly",
   "customer inside at 01:39, outside at 01:40". The model reasons about the gaps between
   sampled frames as if they were cuts. No audio tokens either (DIAL-1 unreachable).
3. **The router is again the better-behaved layer.** Router samples average 6 FPs vs 12.7 for v1
   samples, at similar per-sample legacy score (14.3 vs 13). The router's mirror check produced
   DANC-1, the only defect no v1 sample found; layout-connectivity found HOME-1 in 2 of 3
   samples with clean "second kitchen" reasoning.
4. **Union scoring with additive FP penalties breaks down for low-precision models.** Recall rises
   monotonically with samples (8 → 13 → 14 defects) but FPs grow linearly, so every union
   scores *below* the single runs, the same failure seen on Gemini Pro (09-04: 30/50
   recall, score 1). For the 2.0 track, the single scalar should not be the headline: report
   **union recall** (defects/detection) alongside **per-sample FP rate**, as the 09-04 writeup
   recommended. Proposed 2.0 headline for this model: *14/50 defects, 39/147 detection;
   6.0 FP/router-sample, 12.7 FP/v1-sample.*
   **KT decision (2026-10-27): adopted.** Reports are now recall-first: defects found and
   detection lead, false positives are a separate review-burden column, and the old net score
   survives only as a muted "legacy score". The Pages report shows this table as its 2.0 section.
5. **Single-run noise reconfirmed:** identical v1 runs had legacy scores of 9 / 17 / 13. The leaderboard entry
   (s1) happens to be the worst of the three.
